Keyes Advisory works at the seam between manufacturing and construction, where building systems are engineered, financed, specified, and delivered at scale. A decade spent in design, market development, and venture-backed production means I arrive fluent in the technology, the economics, and the people who move this market.

Modern Methods of Construction

Panelized, volumetric, and hybrid systems. DfMA, factory-to-site logistics, and the delivery models that decide whether offsite economics actually hold.

Mass Timber & Engineered Wood

CLT, glulam, MPP, and timber-hybrid structures, from product development through specification, code pathways, cost benchmarking, and project delivery.

Modular & Prefab Manufacturing

Production strategy, capacity and throughput planning, pricing, and the commercial models that turn a factory into a durable business.

Housing & Public Sector Delivery

Affordable and market housing programs, procurement frameworks, incentive design, and the policy environment that governs how fast adoption moves.
